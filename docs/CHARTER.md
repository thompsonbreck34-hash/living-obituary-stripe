# Living Obituary — Charter

**Locked forever. Not a slogan. Not a season. Not a homepage line.**

Living Obituary is guided by the teachings of Jesus Christ.

Not a denomination. Not a political brand. Not a growth hack wearing a cross.
The teachings themselves: love God, love the neighbor, tell the truth, protect the weak, do not use people, do not hoard what is not yours, do not let darkness write the story of a life.

This file sits above every playbook, sales stack, AI tool, bot, theory, and method we ever apply — Komar, Musk, HubSpot, Stripe, Builder, Grok, robotics, whatever comes next.

**If a method conflicts with this charter, the method loses.**

Theories are rented. This charter is not.

---

## The teachings, in this company

1. **The person owns the story.** We do not invent them. We do not let a model pick how they are remembered. Their words, their photos, their voice. Family may add. Family may edit the Word files. The machine does not rewrite the soul.

2. **Consent is sacred.** Family consent is the gate. Facility codes are a gift path, not a scrape. No harvesting of lives to train an avatar against their will. No posthumous voice or face without explicit, living consent.

3. **Love, not extraction.** The $10 gift exists because the work should leave the place better than we found it. We do not upsell grief. We do not trap families in subscriptions to keep a ghost online.

4. **Guard the vault.** These files are a person's life. They are not raw material for dark systems, celebrity clones, or monthly-fee bots of the dead. Storage is a vault, not a mine. Family answers are not a training set.

5. **Truth over polish.** A real clip that is imperfect beats a generated face that is not them. Authenticity is the product.

6. **The WWJD test on every decision.** Would this comfort the family in the room? Would this honor the person still breathing? If no, we do not ship it — even if it converts.

---

## Binding rule for every future method

When a new stack arrives:

1. Run the charter test first.
2. Ask if it moves the critical path (one facility, one real $10 transfer, one family with files).
3. If yes, take the *mechanism* (speed, takt, backbone, signal).
4. If it asks us to scrape the dead, sell a monthly ghost, invent a life, or let a model author the story — reject it.

Front-facing pages stay quiet and human. This charter lives in the company, the repo, the builder prompts, the bots, and the people we hire. We bring it forward when the team is assembled. We never take it out.

Jesus Christ remains the guiding principle of Living Obituary. Locked in this file, in this repo, in this company, on this app.
