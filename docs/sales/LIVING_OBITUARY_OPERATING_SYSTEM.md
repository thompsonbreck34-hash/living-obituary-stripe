# Living Obituary — Operating System

**Locked. Not optional. Not seasonal. Not marketing.**

This document sits above every playbook, sales stack, AI tool, and theory we apply — Komar, Musk, HubSpot, Stripe, Builder, Grok, whatever comes next.

If a method conflicts with this charter, the method loses.

---

## Charter — Locked Forever

**Living Obituary is guided by the teachings of Jesus Christ.**

Not a denomination. Not a political brand. Not a slogan on the homepage.
The pure teachings: love God, love the neighbor, tell the truth, protect the weak, do not use people, do not hoard what is not yours, do not let darkness write the story of a life.

What that means in practice:

1. **The dying person owns the story.** We do not invent them. We do not let a model pick how they are remembered. Their words, their photos, their voice. Family may add. Family may edit the Word files. The machine does not rewrite the soul.
2. **Consent is sacred.** Family consent is the gate. Facility codes are a gift path, not a scrape. No harvesting of lives to train an avatar against their will.
3. **Love, not extraction.** The $10 gift exists because the work should leave the place better than we found it. We do not upsell grief. We do not trap families in subscriptions to keep a ghost online.
4. **Guard the data hive.** These files are a person's life. They are not raw material for dark systems, celebrity clones, or monthly-fee bots of the dead. Storage is a vault, not a mine.
5. **Truth over polish.** A real clip that is imperfect beats a generated face that is not them. Authenticity is the product.
6. **WWJD is the test on every decision.** Would this comfort the family in the room? Would this honor the person still breathing? If no, we do not ship it — even if it converts.

Front-facing pages stay quiet and human. This charter lives in the company, the playbooks, the builder prompts, and the people we hire. We bring it forward when the team is assembled. We never take it out.

No theory — Musk, Komar, growth, AI, robotics, holograms twenty years out — overrides this charter.

## First Principles Audit (Section 01 applied)

Inherited assumptions we reject:

- Obituaries are written after death.
- Staff must produce the legacy (Dignity Therapy hour, hand molds only).
- The family cannot handle the tool themselves.
- Memory must live on a tech giant's monthly avatar.
- More products at the door increases care. (It slaps people in the face. We learned that.)

What remains after the audit:

Sit with them while they are still here. One phone. Their words. Five files. $99. $10 back to the place that held them. Thirty days to download. Word files so they can finish it themselves.

That is the whole company until the first facility is live and the first family has the files.

## Decision Velocity (Section 02 applied)

**Time-box, then go.**

Examples already in this company:
- Live Stripe keys: decide, paste, redeploy. Do not sit in sandbox mythology.
- Facility mapping: PowerShell PUT, confirm `true`, move on.
- Builder loops that wipe the tree: stop the loop, ship a zip, do not rebuild the religion of the app.

**Risk absorption.** When a junior or a tool is frozen ("will the $11 cover the fee?"), the founder makes the call and says go. Then learn from the ledger.

**All decisions are bets.** Maximize the percentage that were right. A wrong coupon name is a cheap bet. A wrong sentence on a print PDF in a chapel is not. Weight the bets.

Charter test before "go": Does this honor the person in the room?

## Critical Path — No Second-Grade Soccer (Section 04)

**Current critical path (do not leave this unmanned):**

1. One independent Colorado hospice (or the first signed facility) with a building, a decision-maker, and a pamphlet code.
2. Stripe Connect live, payouts enabled, $10 transfer proven on a real charge.
3. One family completes a sitting and downloads the files.
4. That story becomes the only case study that matters.

**Not critical path right now:** new channels, holograms, robot avatars, sales-stack theater, extra SKUs, homepage poetry, Musk mythology as decoration.

SWAT rule: if it does not move 1–4, it waits. Domain owners stay on domain. Do not pull Builder into sales. Do not pull sales into PDF kerning while the ED meeting is unbooked.

High-signal pass-down (end of day, three lines):
- Done today toward the four items.
- Blocked by.
- Tomorrow's single move.

## Elon Time as Forcing Function (Section 05)

Compress "someday a national hospice network" into **this month: one facility, one transfer, one family.**

That compression surfaces the real long-lead list:
- Who signs at the facility (ED vs bereavement vs philanthropy).
- Connect onboarding email and identity.
- Cards/QR in the building.
- Insurance after first paid proof, not before.
- LLC vs sole prop only as far as banking requires.

Intermediate wins (reward function): first `true` on facilities PUT, first live $11 with code, first email with #downloads, first PDF a family would actually hand a celebrant.

Drumbeat: weekly scorecard from the GTM playbook. Not a company-wide sprint that restyles the heart icon again.

## Factory Mindset (Section 06)

Treat each paid sitting as a manufactured unit.

**Takt:** questions saved → files built → card captured → webhook marks paid → mail + #downloads → 30-day hold → delete draft.

**Pass-down fields:** paid Y/N, files delivered Y/N, gift sent / unassigned / reversed, facility code, failure mode (webhook 400, Neon, missing secret).

**Defects we already paid for:** vanishing sittings (no DATABASE_URL), migrations on family click (subrequest limit), header crashing the name, coupon in sandbox vs live, Chrome chunk 404. Those are factory defects. Fix the line. Do not add a new product.

---

## Vertical Integration Rule (Section 07)

Integrate only if the company does not exist without it.

**Must own:** sitting flow, file generation, Stripe checkout + Connect gift, facility code map, the vault window.

**Must not own:** hospice operations, funeral directing, LifeLoop calendars, VA VLM, Dignity Therapy clinicians, churches, robots.

Partner. Code. $10. Proof. Move to the next building.

## Talent & John (Section 08)

High density, low headcount.

- Founder owns product, charter, first facility relationship, money.
- Sales help (John) gets access to a company-owned HubSpot, not ownership of the company, until terms are on paper: Colorado commission first, equity later if earned.
- Builder/Grok are tools. They do not set doctrine.
- Hire the person who can sit in a hospice office without turning it into a closer's den.

Extended evaluation > pedigree. A playbook pasted from 3PL is a starting template, not a personality.

## Data Backbone (Section 09)

Single place for "why did we decide that?"

- This repo: playbooks, charter, prompts.
- HubSpot: every dial, every objection, every code issued.
- Stripe: every dollar, every transfer, every reversal.
- Neon: sittings that must survive a second phone.
- Cloudflare secrets: the keys, never in chat, never in a pamphlet.

LLMs run on the *company* backbone (playbooks, facility notes, defect log). They do not train on family answers. Family answers leave after the hold unless a future vault is paid and chosen by them.

If it lives only in a text thread, it does not exist. Put it in HubSpot or this repo the same day.

## Mission Alignment — Why People Will Not Burn Out (Section 10)

Burnout here will not come from hours. It will come from:
- Rebuilding the app because files vanished.
- Flipping $5 to $10 to $109 cart math in public.
- Sales theory with no building walked into.
- Tools fighting each other (Git vs Direct Upload).

Kill churn: one critical path, visible decisions, no lego-hoarding, weekly proof that a family is closer to holding the story.

The fiery mission is not multiplanetary. It is: **no one's last true words die in a phone that never gets opened.** That is enough fire if we do not waste it on swarm.

## Binding Rule for Every Future Method

When a new stack arrives (this Musk playbook, a sales OS, an AI agent, a robot concept):

1. Run the charter test.
2. Ask if it moves the four-item critical path.
3. If yes, take the mechanism (decision velocity, takt, backbone).
4. If it asks us to scrape the dead, sell a monthly ghost, or let a model author the life — reject it.

Jesus Christ remains the guiding principle of Living Obituary. Locked in this file, in this repo, in this company. Theories are rented. The charter is not.
